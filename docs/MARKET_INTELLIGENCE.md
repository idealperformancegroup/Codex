# Market + Review Intelligence v1

This layer turns sourced competitor observations and customer reviews into structured evidence.

## Competitor evidence
Each observation preserves its source URL. Price ranges are calculated only from observed examples and are not represented as market share or category-wide truth.

## Review mining
Review themes are descriptive. A theme must occur more than once before it is called recurring. The engine preserves examples and source URLs so a human can audit the interpretation.

## Unit economics
Economics are scenario calculations. Every number is an assumption until supported by a source or first-party test data.

## Opportunity thesis
The thesis joins social-signal intelligence, competitor evidence, and recurring customer complaints. It produces gap hypotheses rather than automatically approving a business.

The next layer will automate evidence collection through research adapters and convert an approved thesis into the smallest credible experiment.
