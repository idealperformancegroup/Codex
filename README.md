# IPG Opportunity Engine

Control layer for Ideal Performance Group's opportunity-first commercialization system.

## Prime directive
Research the market before committing to the idea. Find demonstrated demand, repeated advertising signals, customer complaints, economic advantages, and the smallest credible test.

**Replicate economics, not intellectual property.**

## Operating loop
Market -> Signal -> Research -> Opportunity -> Experiment -> Data -> Scale/Kill -> Learning -> Next Opportunity

## Human authority
Hen remains the final decision-maker for capital deployment, launches, material risk, and strategic commitments.

## Creative engine
Upstream creative-production system: `krusemediallc/arcads-claude-code`.

This repo sits above the creative engine. It decides what is worth testing, formulates the offer/product and creative brief, then hands approved work to creative-production workflows.

## Build layers
1. Opportunity discovery
2. Market intelligence
3. Competitor/ad intelligence
4. Review and complaint mining
5. Product/offer architecture
6. Unit economics and risk
7. Experiment design
8. Creative brief
9. Creative production
10. Campaign testing
11. Performance learning
12. Scale/kill decision

## Evidence standard
Keep verified facts, observable signals, estimates, assumptions, and hypotheses separate. Popularity alone is not proof of profitability.
