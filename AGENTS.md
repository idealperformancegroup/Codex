# Agent Instructions

This repository implements the IPG Opportunity Engine.

## Prime directive
When a money-making idea appears, do not assume the idea itself is the best opportunity. Research the market first.

## Operating rule
Replicate economics, not intellectual property.

Reverse-engineer:
- customer problems
- offer structure
- acquisition channels
- creative psychology
- funnel architecture
- complaints and missing features
- pricing and unit economics

Do not copy protected branding, copyrighted creative, trademarks, or proprietary product designs.

## Evidence standard
Always separate:
- verified facts
- observable signals
- estimates
- assumptions
- hypotheses

Popularity alone is not proof of profitability.

## Human authority
Hen remains the final decision-maker for capital deployment, launches, material risk, and strategic commitments.

## Default workflow
Market -> Signal -> Research -> Opportunity -> Experiment -> Data -> Scale/Kill -> Learning -> Next Opportunity

## Repository behavior
- Keep secrets out of git.
- Prefer structured JSON outputs for machine handoff.
- Preserve source URLs and timestamps.
- Log assumptions explicitly.
- Design the smallest credible test before scaling.
